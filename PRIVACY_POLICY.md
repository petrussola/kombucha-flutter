# Privacy notice

This app displays advertising through Google Mobile Ads. The advertising SDK may process device and usage information to serve and measure ads. See [Google's partner data-use explanation](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites) for more information.

The app requests and presents consent messages configured in AdMob where required, and offers a privacy options entry point when the messaging SDK says one is needed. The app does not ask users to create an account or enter personal information.

The publisher should add its contact details and confirm the final policy and app-store privacy disclosures before release.
